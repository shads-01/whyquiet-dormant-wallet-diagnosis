## A. Customers and fees

### A1. Cash-out is expensive, and customers are working around it
- Role: ordinary customers; also agents on the losing side
- Quote: [NO SOURCE — secondary reporting, no interviewee statement recorded]
- What they did / what happened: cashing out Tk 30,000 costs at least Tk 387. Many customers now take cash from merchants via Bangla QR and pay no fee. Industry sources report a Tk 10,000 QR payment returning roughly Tk 9,815 in cash, with the merchant keeping a cash-out-like cut. The central bank has warned against transaction splitting and cash-out misuse.
- Cost: Tk 387+ per Tk 30,000. Normal cash-out is about Tk 13 to 18.50 per Tk 1,000. Upay says it bears about Tk 5 to 8 per Tk 1,000 when its customers pay through another operator's QR. For comparison TIB found Tk 25,000 cash-out costs Tk 372.5 to 462.5 in Bangladesh, and is free in India (Pakistan Tk 355.7, Myanmar Tk 231.3). Banks charge at most Tk 29 for the same amount.
- Emotion: [NO SOURCE]
- Evidence: https://en.prothomalo.com/business/local/8mljzomg86 (Sept 2026); https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/last-page/bangla-qr-misuse-raises-concerns-over-mfs-sustainability-1789410537 (Sept 2026); https://www.researchgate.net/publication/392707323_Governance_Challenges_in_Mobile_Financial_Services_Sector_in_Bangladesh (2025); https://tbsnews.net/supplement/p2p-bangla-qr-could-reshape-bangladeshs-digital-payment-ecosystem-1556991 (Sept 2026)
- Status: UNSOLVED. Regulator is debating; Bangla QR IRF is zero and MDR is capped at 1.15%, versus about 1.85% for cash-out.

### A2. Fraud victims get slow or no redress, and then leave the platform
- Role: customers
- Quote: [NO SOURCE — survey aggregates only, no verbatim statements recorded]
- What they did / what happened: PRI's 2021 survey of 7,279 users found about 9.3% had been defrauded, mostly through compromised PINs or impersonation. About 30% never had their complaint resolved. Victims whose complaint was solved within a day mostly kept using MFS; those never looked into abandoned it. TIB (2025) found 58.8% of victim users did not file any complaint, only 38.1% of those who did got it resolved, and only 6.2% knew about Bangladesh Bank's complaint centre (CIPC).
- Cost: average loss about Tk 9,000 (PRI). TIB: user losses Tk 300 to 83,000. About 32% of non-users avoid MFS from fear of fraud. Time cost not reported.
- Emotion: [NO SOURCE]
- Evidence: https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/crime-justice/news/one-10-mfs-users-victims-fraud-2994376 (2021 survey); https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/mfs-enabling-money-transfers-also-abuses-3904931 (May 2025)
- Status: UNSOLVED. Providers run hotlines, but users report them ineffective.

### A3. Criminals social-engineer the provider's own customer care (SIM replacement plus account recovery)
- Role: business owners (victims)
- Quote: [NO SOURCE — police case reported without victim statements]
- What they did / what happened: police in Bagerhat arrested nine people this week. The gang allegedly used cloned fingerprints to get replacement SIMs, then called customer care claiming the account was blocked, with prepared answers to the security questions. Care staff restored access.
- Cost: Tk 2.5 lakh stolen from two businessmen's bKash and Nagad accounts.
- Emotion: [NO SOURCE]
- Evidence: https://tbsnews.net/bangladesh/crime/9-arrested-bagerhat-over-sim-cloning-mobile-banking-fraud-1557496 (Oct 2026)
- Status: UNSOLVED (police case, current). One case; look for a pattern in interviews.

### A4. Accounts frozen at scale; legitimate users risk getting caught
- Role: customers, agents
- Quote: [NO SOURCE]
- What they did / what happened: BFIU has frozen or suspended about 55,000 MFS accounts for gambling and digital hundi, and began freezing about 14,000 more in August 2026, while moving to block those NIDs from opening new accounts. In a 2021 case, Nagad customers said their accounts were suspended despite proof of valid transactions. TIB says offenders simply re-register with new details.
- Cost: a frozen balance and no cash-out. Amounts not reported. Duration of freeze not reported.
- Emotion: [NO SOURCE]
- Evidence: https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/bfiu-freezes-55000-mfs-accounts-over-gambling-hundi-allegations-khosru-1471416 (June 2026); https://www.yogonet.com/international/news/2026/08/04/125711-bangladesh-moves-to-freeze-14-000-more-mobile-accounts-linked-to-online-gambling (Aug 2026); https://www.thedailystar.net/business/economy/banks/news/nagad-customers-accounts-blocked-suspicious-activity-2171026 (2021)
- Gap: I found no 2026 reporting on wrongly frozen individuals. This is a question for interviews.

### A5. Women often lack their own wallet and depend on agents
- Role: women, including working women
- Quote: [NO SOURCE]
- What they did / what happened: 82.58% of men have MFS accounts versus 47.16% of women (Dhaka University study). Working women often use a father's, husband's or brother's account or transact directly through agents, and SIMs are often registered in a male relative's name.
- Cost: not reported. Fee cost falls on the account holder; unquantified here.
- Emotion: [NO SOURCE]
- Evidence: https://www.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/gender-gap-mfs-widens-3-16-five-years-3573356 (2023); https://digitalfrontiersinstitute.org/digital-financial-inclusion-of-working-women-a-deep-look-in-the-district-of-sylhet-bangladesh/ (2025)
- Untested hypothesis, not from a source: agent dependence may raise PIN exposure. Ask about it.

## B. Agents

### B1. Agent income is shrinking and fraud losses fall on agents
- Role: agents
- Quote: [NO SOURCE]
- What they did / what happened: of a cash-out fee, the agent, distributor and mobile operator together receive at least Tk 6.40 per Tk 1,000. As cash-out declines, many agents are losing interest. PRI: about 13% of agents had faced fraud, with average loss over Tk 18,000, and about 45% said complaints were never resolved. TIB: 17% of agents were victims (losses Tk 200 to 3.76 lakh), and most agents are not regularly audited.
- Cost: at least Tk 6.40 per Tk 1,000 of the cash-out fee is the agent/distributor/operator share. Fraud losses: average over Tk 18,000 (PRI); Tk 200 to 3.76 lakh (TIB). Complaint-resolution time not reported.
- Emotion: [NO SOURCE — "losing interest" is reported behaviour, not a reported feeling]
- Evidence: https://en.prothomalo.com/business/local/8mljzomg86 (Sept 2026); https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/home/scam-shooting-sought-as-mfs-transactions-boom-1648695321 (2021); https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/mfs-enabling-money-transfers-also-abuses-3904931 (2025)
- Status: UNSOLVED.

### B2. Float and settlement shocks (holidays, elections, banking hours)
- Role: agents, distributors, customers
- Quote: [NO SOURCE — Dhaka agents' frustration reported, not quoted]
- What they did / what happened: settlement with banks has to fit limited banking hours (the 2020 account cites 10am to 1pm). In the February 2026 election restrictions, Dhaka agents said cash-out was suspended and customers were frustrated. Activists could not receive small allowances and relied on credit.
- Cost: small allowances went unpaid and were replaced by credit; amount not reported. Banking window is 3 hours a day in the cited case.
- Emotion: [NO SOURCE — "frustrated" is reported as narration, not a captured statement]
- Evidence: https://www.thedailystar.net/toggle/news/mobile-financial-services-soaring-pandemic-stricken-bangladesh-1900507 (2020); https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/banking/mfs-agents-report-customer-frustration-amid-96-hour-restrictions-1358156 (Feb 2026)
- Status: PARTLY SOLVED. Bangladesh Bank allowed weekend and holiday distributor cash transactions (up to Tk 50 lakh per day, 2022): https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/fund-transfer-among-mfs-distributors-become-easy-3110836. Human runners (DSRs) feed agents liquidity. Remaining pain is shocks and the cost of running it.

## C. Merchants and shopkeepers

### C1. Digital money cannot move upstream, so retailers cash out to restock
- Role: shopkeeper (example in source: Manikganj), wholesaler, distributor
- Quote: [NO SOURCE]
- What they did / what happened: a retailer receives a QR payment, then withdraws from an agent paying MDR plus an agent fee, because the wholesaler demands cash. MicroSave found about 37% of retailers avoid digital payments for this reason, and only about 10% of flows beyond the retail counter stay digital. A shopkeeper's margin is about Tk 5 to 10 per Tk 100. A distributor's is about Tk 1 to 2, so a roughly 1% fee takes half or more. Distributors also need order-matched confirmation and partial-payment support, which wallets lack.
- Cost: MDR plus agent fee per round trip; a cash-handling burden on 7.8 million small retailers. On a distributor's Tk 1 to 2 margin per Tk 100, a roughly 1% fee consumes half or more of it.
- Emotion: [NO SOURCE]
- Evidence: https://www.microsave.net/2026/05/14/why-bangladeshs-supply-chains-still-run-on-cash-despite-digitization/ (May 2026)
- Status: UNSOLVED per MicroSave.

### C2. Merchant status is penalised; agent status pays
- Role: small trader
- Quote: [NO SOURCE]
- What they did / what happened: registering as a Bangla QR merchant means MDR deductions, while the same shop as an agent earns commissions. MDR of 1.15% is cited as a deterrent for micro-merchants. Cash is still about 35% of formal retail payments.
- Cost: MDR of 1.15% deducted on turnover. Cash still about 35% of formal retail payments.
- Emotion: [NO SOURCE]
- Evidence: https://www.dhakatribune.com/opinion/op-ed/413856/digital-payment-boon-or-bane (2026); https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/views/views/overcoming-the-adoption-barriers (2025)
- Status: IN PROGRESS. From 1 Oct 2026, instant settlement, no 1% minimum MDR, and zero IRF: https://thedailystar.net/business/news/bangla-qr-payments-become-cheaper-instant-merchants-tomorrow-bb-4286986. Treat as being solved.

### C3. Refunds stuck when a seller disappears or an upstream account is frozen
- Role: online buyers
- Quote: [NO SOURCE]
- What they did / what happened: Tk 214 crore was stuck in payment gateways in 2021, of which Tk 166 crore was frozen at one gateway on a police request. The High Court asked why the authorities' inaction should not be declared illegal. Many buyers said they got neither product nor refund.
- Cost: Tk 214 crore stuck in 2021, of which Tk 166 crore frozen at one gateway. Individual refund amounts and wait time not reported.
- Emotion: [NO SOURCE]
- Evidence: https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/uncertainty-over-refund-e-commerce-payment-gateways-324001 (2021)
- Gap: I could not verify the Tk 58 crore figure in the earlier plan. The sourced number is Tk 214 crore, dated 2021. Check the current state before using it.
