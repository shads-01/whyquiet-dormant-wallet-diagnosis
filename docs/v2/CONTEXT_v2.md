# CONTEXT v2 (overrides conflicting parts of docs/CONTEXT.md; all other rules there still apply)
Goal: WIN the DIU CPC x upay AI Hackathon with a 3-person team in 48 hours, not publish a paper. Winning means a judge says "I haven't seen this" within 30 seconds and an upay executive would pilot it next quarter.
1. PROBLEM FIRST. Start from documented, costly, unsolved problems and from field interviews. Data comes second.
2. TWO VALID LANES.
 Lane A: a learned model on real public or collected data.
 Lane B: a mechanism, system or market product where AI makes a core decision inside a new incentive, protocol, workflow, marketplace or network response. It is evaluated with a transparent simulation calibrated to REAL public numbers (fees, prices, volumes, loss figures). Claims are about economics and outcomes under stated assumptions, never about accuracy on real people. Labelled synthetic data is allowed in this lane.
3. ALLOWED KILL REASONS ONLY: (a) ALREADY SOLVED in Bangladesh, with a URL showing a working product; (b) BLOCKED by a named law or regulator; (c) NOBODY WOULD USE IT, with named evidence. Small sample, weak labels, unproven, hard to build or "an LLM could do it" are FIX ITEMS, not kills.
4. FEASIBILITY IS A GATE (a plausible 48-hour demo path exists, P >= 0.5), not a multiplier. Rank survivors by UPSIDE = problem relevance x business impact x originality.
5. PITCH TEST for every finalist: one sentence a judge repeats to a colleague; one number in taka the executive remembers; one live demo moment.
6. Ambition is expected: at least half the finalists must change a mechanism (incentive, settlement, protocol, who pays, who decides), not add a model to an existing screen.
