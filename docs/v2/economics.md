DETAIL PER IDEA
======================================================================================================================

[1] 1. Women's balance: no merchant cash-out
    mechanism : Changes a settlement rule: a named cohort's balance cannot be cashed at a merchant QR.
    who pays  : Nobody pays for the rule. It is a settlement constraint upay publishes unilaterally. upay forgoes nothing it books today (a disguised cash-out earns upay no MDR and, cross-operator, costs it Tk5-8/1,000).
    per unit  : per shifted active woman: Tk51/yr revenue retained + up to Tk201 one-off CAC avoided
    unit(soc) : privacy and dignity; not priced anywhere
    upay gross Tk cr: cons 0.06 | base 0.57 | aggressive 3.17
    upay NET   Tk cr: cons 0.01 | base 0.52 | aggressive 3.12   (opex 0.05 / 0.05 / 0.05)
    break-even: 0.263% of active women shifted (~3,298 users). Near zero, because the rule costs almost nothing.
    top 2 keys: A13 wallet-sharing rate (unsourced)
               A4 Tk201 CAC proxy (2021 launch budget, not a marginal CAC)
    VERDICT   : Cheap and real, but NOT a taka story. Revenue-only view is Tk0.06-0.64cr. The headline number is acquisition-cost avoidance, and that number rests on a launch-budget proxy. Judge pitch should NOT use a taka figure for this idea; use the retention one.

[2] 2. Credit passport (agency sale of record)
    mechanism : Changes who decides eligibility: the borrower's own repayment ledger, shown back to them and sold as evidence.
    who pays  : A BB-licensed bank or NBFC pays, on loans originated through the passport. upay is the agent, not the lender - the role BB MFS Guidelines cl. 6.6 explicitly permits.
    per unit  : per borrower originated: Tk100 / Tk480 / Tk900 (1/2/3% of a Tk10k/24k/30k loan)
    unit(soc) : per borrower: Tk57,000 of avoided arrears (n=1, Prothom Alo)
    upay gross Tk cr: cons 0.15 | base 2.86 | aggressive 13.39
    upay NET   Tk cr: cons -1.35 | base 1.36 | aggressive 11.89   (opex 1.50 / 1.50 / 1.50)
    break-even: 31,250 borrowers/yr = 1.050% of upay's active base
    top 2 keys: A15 share of active customers who used an unlicensed app (unsourced)
               A14 agency fee rate (no such product exists, so no market rate)
    VERDICT   : Base case Tk2.86cr = 7% of upay revenue, against a social pool ~13x larger. The economics are small; the compliance exposure is the real risk, not the upside.

[3] 3. Not-yet-sent (pre-execution hold)
    mechanism : Changes when irreversibility happens: before settlement, not after. Nothing settled is ever reversed (PSS Act 2024 finality).
    who pays  : Nobody. upay PAYS: a goodwill reimbursement budget plus review ops. The recovered taka belongs to the victim, not to upay, and upay cannot book it. A protection fee on the sender would need BB prior approval as a customer charge.
    per unit  : per hold-eligible send: Tk54 / Tk162 / Tk432 of VICTIM loss avoided
    unit(soc) : per stopped fraud: Tk9,000 retained by the victim (PRI 2022 mean; TIB 2025 publishes only a Tk300-Tk83,000 range)
    upay gross Tk cr: cons 0 | base 0 | aggressive 0
    upay NET   Tk cr: cons -0.50 | base -1.50 | aggressive -4.00   (opex 0.50 / 1.50 / 4.00)
    break-even: NEVER on upay's P&L: saving Tk0 to upay, cost Tk0.5-4.0cr. On a social-cost basis, Tk1.5cr of ops buys Tk6.18cr of victim recovery (4x).
    top 2 keys: A10 per-send fraud incidence (unsourced; weakest number in the model)
               A11 pre-vs-post recovery delta (critique's 75%/19% pair had NO source and is dropped)
    VERDICT   : CORRECTION TO critique.md. Its pitch number 'Tk9,000 -> Tk6,750 per stopped transfer' is victim economics, not upay economics. upay's annual P&L impact is NEGATIVE. Do not put an upay taka number on stage for this one.

[4] 4. QR payee-identity label at scan time
    mechanism : Changes who decides: the customer, at scan time, instead of the platform, after the fact.
    who pays  : Nobody. It is loss prevention and enforcement targeting under BB's Apr 2026 circular, which already orders cancellation of cash-out merchants - the model decides WHICH points get de-registered.
    per unit  : per Tk1,000 of DISGUISED cross-operator QR prevented: Tk6.50 (upay's own stated Tk5-8/1,000 cost, midpoint)
    unit(soc) : merchants de-registered are the only agents who lose; labelling loses no merchant (bKash removed 7,000+ points instead)
    upay gross Tk cr: cons 0.0048 | base 0.10 | aggressive 0.57
    upay NET   Tk cr: cons -0.20 | base -0.70 | aggressive -0.93   (opex 0.20 / 0.80 / 1.50)
    break-even: needs Tk123cr/yr of disguised cross-op QR prevented = 31% of upay's ENTIRE transaction value. NOT ACHIEVABLE at op cost Tk0.8cr. Even at a stripped-down Tk0.2cr op cost it needs 8% - a stretch. Put differently: upay's TOTAL cross-operator QR loss, if every cross-op taka were a disguised cash-out, is Tk6.39cr at the aggressive volume assumption, against a Tk0.8cr cost.
    top 2 keys: A7 cross-operator QR share of upay volume (upay publishes no QR split)
               A8 disguised-cash-out share (only public proxy is Nagad's 12.1% off-hours share of its own BDQR, 29 days)
    VERDICT   : FAILS ITS OWN BREAK-EVEN. upay's ENTIRE cross-operator QR cost ceiling is Tk0.64-6.39cr/yr - the problem is real but at upay's 0.5% volume share it is not a P&L event. Salvage as a compliance/BB-relationship story, not a savings story.

[5] 5. Consignment QR + khata escrow
    mechanism : Changes settlement: payment and goods become one object, so the distributor's goods release is backed by evidence rather than a bag of notes. Escrow is a TSA question under PSS Act 2024 / PSO Regulation 2025 and must be resolved before build.
    who pays  : Nobody pays upay a new fee - upay earns ordinary MDR on wholesale volume that is currently 100% cash. The distributor's preserved margin (Tk1.50 per Tk100) is the value created and it belongs to the distributor.
    per unit  : per Tk100 of wholesale: upay earns Tk0.50 MDR; distributor keeps Tk1.50 of margin
    unit(soc) : Tk15.00 of distributor margin preserved per Tk1,000 (midpoint of BDT1-2 per BDT100, MicroSave)
    upay gross Tk cr: cons 0.07 | base 1.44 | aggressive 18.00
    upay NET   Tk cr: cons -0.93 | base 0.44 | aggressive 17.00   (opex 1.00 / 1.00 / 1.00)
    break-even: Tk200cr/yr of wholesale volume = 1,389 retailers at Tk120,000/month. Modest - and the Agrani Distribution B2B channel announced Aug 2026 is the obvious route, but it discloses no volume.
    top 2 keys: A17 retailers reached (upay has 14,519 merchants total; Agrani volume undisclosed)
               A18 average wholesale ticket per retailer (no BD figure exists)
    VERDICT   : The only idea here with a real revenue line, but it is a channel business whose size depends on a partnership nobody has quantified. Note the timing: MicroSave's blocker (a 1% MDR eating half a distributor's 1-2% margin) was REMOVED BY REGULATION on 1 Oct 2026. The remaining blocker is settlement confirmation - which is exactly what this sells. That is a genuinely better story than problems.md told.

[6] 6. Agent float priced as a public utility
    mechanism : Changes an incentive: float goes from a free allocation to a priced, traded capacity. BB's Feb 2026 96-hour decree rationed cash-out by fiat for 96 hours, which is the scene-setting fact.
    who pays  : Agents pay, for guaranteed next-day cash. upay earns a clearing fee on allocated float. LEGAL RISK: a charge on agents is a levy and needs BB prior approval under the PSO Regulation 2025.
    per unit  : per taka of float per day: Tk0.50 / Tk1.50 / Tk3.00 per Tk1,000 (no market reference price exists)
    unit(soc) : float efficiency for upay: on Tk6.33cr of float at a ~1% yield = Tk0.06cr/yr. Negligible.
    upay gross Tk cr: cons 0.03 | base 0.52 | aggressive 4.85
    upay NET   Tk cr: cons -0.57 | base -0.08 | aggressive 4.25   (opex 0.60 / 0.60 / 0.60)
    break-even: needs Tk1.10cr of float traded per day at the base fee. Base case trades Tk0.95cr/day - SHORT BY 15%. The fee rate is the entire ballgame.
    top 2 keys: A21 share of agents who would bid for float (NO evidence found that float is scarce or contested - critique residual risk #4, unresolved)
               A22 clearing fee rate (no reference price exists anywhere)
    VERDICT   : Base case Tk0.52cr sits just BELOW its own break-even. The scene (a central bank rationing cash-out by decree) is verified and strong; the premise (that float is a tradable scarce good) is NOT. problems.md itself rated P9 U=3, conceding float shocks are 'partly fixed'. No source establishes that agents bid for float.

CORRECTIONS TO THE INPUT DOCS (found while sourcing)
======================================================================================================================

  * problems.md: 'agents take >=Tk6.40 per Tk1,000'
      WRONG. Tk6.40/1,000 is the MFS PROVIDER'S COST for cash-in, per TBS. The agent+distributor pool is 77% of the cash-out charge = Tk14.25/1,000 at Tk18.50, of which the retail agent's own take is ~Tk9+. This number is load-bearing for ideas 5 and 6 in critique.md.

  * problems.md: '~2.4m agents'
      WRONG. BB reports 1,856,190 MFS agents (Feb 2025). '2.4m' appears to be a slide of 239.24m ACCOUNTS. Agent-banking agents are a separate, much smaller channel: 15,838 (Mar 2025).

  * problems.md: 'avg loss ~Tk9,000' attributed to TIB
      MISATTRIBUTED. Tk9,000 is PRI's 2022 MEAN (n=7,279). TIB's 2025 study publishes only a RANGE, Tk300-Tk83,000, with no mean. Two different surveys were merged.

  * problems.md: 'cash-out rate ~1.85%'
      ARITHMETICALLY OK but soft. It is 1 - 9,815/10,000 from ONE reported merchant-QR cash-out instance, and 9,815 is an industry-source estimate, not a published average. Normal cash-out is Tk13-18.50/1,000 = 1.3-1.85%.

  * critique.md: '75% recovery inside 1-2h vs 19% after'
      NO SOURCE. No publication states these figures. Dropped. Replaced by A11 (30/45/60pp), which is weaker but labelled.

  * critique.md: '16,000 agent-banking outlets'
      MINOR. BB Mar 2025: 15,838 agents operating 21,023 outlets. Also, the 50% female mandate is a BRPD circular of 8 May 2025; female share of agent-banking agents was 9.11% at Dec 2024.

  * 'BDQR Tk370m -> Tk1.1bn' (July->Aug 2026, daily)
      UNRECONCILED with UNB's 30 Sep 2026 figure of Tk143.54 CRORE/day from Tk28 crore in June. The two imply a 13x jump in one month. Both are carried; neither is used as a primary input, because neither is needed (upay's QR volume is modelled off upay's own value, not BDQR's).

  * MicroSave's 1% MDR blocker for upstream digitisation
      OBSOLETE as of 1 Oct 2026. BB removed the 1% MDR floor and set IRF to zero. The fee blocker is legislated away; settlement CONFIRMATION is now the whole blocker. This strengthens idea 5 materially.

  A1. ASSUMPTION: upay's active-customer rate is 35% of registered. [nearest source: TBS_UPAY_2023, TI_BD_MFS_2025]

      No upay P&L for 2024 or 2025 is public. If upay has grown, every absolute taka figure here is too low; if not, they are about right.

      Derived: Tk43.32cr revenue / Tk9,826cr volume. This is upay's realised revenue yield, not a posted rate.

  A4. ASSUMPTION: upay's customer acquisition cost is Tk201 per net customer. [nearest source: MARKEDIUM_UPAY_2021]
      Derived: 2021 selling & marketing Tk785.0m / 3.9m cumulative customers.   A5. ASSUMPTION: Average MFS transaction ticket is Tk2,328. [nearest source: FE_OCT2025]

      30% P2P, 23.5% cash-out by value. upay's own 2021 split was 98.5% revenue from cash-out & others, so upay is more cash-out-heavy than assumed here. This makes the P2P-derived numbers for idea 3 an OVERSTATEMENT.
