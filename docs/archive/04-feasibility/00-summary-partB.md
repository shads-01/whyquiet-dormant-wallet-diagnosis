## Cross-cutting findings

1. **All four datasets/competitor claims from earlier rounds now verified with URLs** — including the two that anchor positioning: Google Messages on-device scam detection (Mar 2025) and Truecaller Scam Checker both exist and are free, so per-message scam *detection* is already-dominant; the census/forensics layers are the defensible slivers.
2. **Legal baseline is better than feared and now sourced:** no all-party consent statute for participant recording; PDPA 2026 (Act No. 63) makes voiceprints biometric data and requires consent for our processing; Telecom Act s.70(3) criminalizes AI voice imitation to harm — the Agent Scam Drill must use anonymous corpus voices, never cloned ones. Source: recordinglaw.com Bangladesh page (cites bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd), opened 2026-10-02.
3. **The two strongest measured signals this pass:** (a) naive baselines on noisy Bangla text are near-zero (0.20 acc / 0.00 F1) → trained models genuinely add value in ideas 05; (b) zero-shot ASR on dialects is catastrophically bad (WER 7.48) → idea 06's fine-tune is the whole project, and the menu-IVR fallback should be built in parallel.
